About Cedar3 Technology

A proudly South African technical services company, committed to excellence in office equipment and IT support.

Our Story

Cedar3 Technology (Pty) Ltd was founded on a simple observation: when a business printer or workstation fails, the whole office slows down. Downtime costs money, frustrates staff, and hurts customer service. We built our workshop in Benoni South to give local businesses a faster, more reliable option than waiting on distant service contracts.

Today we support corporate, SME, and private clients across the East Rand and Johannesburg with on-site and workshop-based repairs, preventative maintenance, and hardware supply. Every job — from a single desktop to a fleet of multifunction printers — is handled with the same commitment to excellence.
